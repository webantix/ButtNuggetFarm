Hatching tips & FAQ

How should I store eggs before setting them?

Pointy end down, somewhere cool (about 13–16°C) and out of the sun. Tilt or turn them daily and set within 7 days. Let them rest 12–24 hours after the drive home.

How long do they take to hatch?

21 days. Stop turning at day 18 and raise the humidity for lockdown. A day early or late is normal.

What does "straight run" mean?

Unsexed. You'll get a natural mix of hens and roosters, often about half and half. Have a plan for roosters and check your council's rules first.

Do you guarantee hatch rates?

No. Our eggs are fertile when they leave the farm, but transport, storage and your incubator all affect the hatch, and those are out of our hands.

How do I look after chicks in the first weeks?

A draught-free brooder at 32–35°C in week one, about 3°C cooler each week after. Chick starter crumble and clean, shallow water every day.

Do you post eggs?

No. Everything is farm pickup only in Gidgegannup. No postage, delivery or meet-ups.
